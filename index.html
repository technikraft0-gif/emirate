07 — Questions

Frequently asked details.

Everything you need to know about timelines, contracts, ownership, and our collaboration cadence.

Still curious or have custom requirements?

Direct inbox response within 24 hours.

hello@emirate.design ↗
How long does a brand identity project typically take?

Full brand identity sprints typically take 4 to 5 weeks from kick-off to final delivery. Starter logo projects take approximately 2 to 3 weeks. Fast-track timelines are available for time-sensitive funding announcements.

How do revisions and feedback cycles work?

Every package includes two comprehensive rounds of revisions on the selected direction. Because we spend substantial time aligning on strategy and moodboards during Week 1, our directions consistently hit the mark without endless back-and-forth.

What are the payment and deposit terms?

We operate on a 50% deposit upon contract signing to lock in the production slot, and the remaining 50% upon final sign-off prior to releasing master production files. Bank wire and major credit cards accepted.

What file formats will I receive upon completion?

You receive an organized, permanent cloud delivery archive containing raw vector files (AI, EPS, SVG), digital formats (PNG, JPG, WebP), color modes (RGB, CMYK, Pantone specs), native Figma components, and print-ready PDF packages.

Do you work with international clients across timezones?

Yes, over 70% of our client base is located in North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. We utilize asynchronous Loom video walkthroughs, shared Figma canvases, and flexible call scheduling to make collaboration effortless.

Do I own 100% of the rights to the completed designs?

Absolutely. Upon final payment settlement, full copyright and worldwide commercial intellectual property rights are officially transferred to your entity.
